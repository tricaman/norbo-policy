About the service

Norbo is a mobile application for managing the lives of domestic and non-domestic animals. It lets you, among other things:

  • Record information about your animals (dogs, cats, fish, birds, reptiles, small mammals, amphibians, invertebrates, equines, farm animals, and other categories);
  • Keep a timeline of health and life events (vaccinations, visits, weight, environmental parameters, photos);
  • Set reminders for health due dates, maintenance tasks, document renewals, and milestones;
  • Keep a photo album and a record of your expenses;
  • Browse general care information by animal category.

Norbo is a personal organization tool. It is not a veterinary service or a substitute for one.
